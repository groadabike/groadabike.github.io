Hi,
I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in the Acoustics Research Group at the University of Salford working on 
[The Cadenza Project: Improving Music Listening for those with Hearing Impairment](http://cadenzachallenge.org/).

I hold a PhD in Computer Science from [The University of Sheffield](http://www.sheffield.ac.uk) under the 
supervision of [Professor Jon Barker](https://www.sheffield.ac.uk/dcs/people/academic/jbarker).
In my project, I worked on the automatic sung speech recognition task, also known as automatic lyrics transcription.
For this, I first studied the key differences between sung and spoken speech that make the former more difficult to recognise.
Then, I used these differences to inform and adapt deep learning technologies designed for spoken speech to sung speech.

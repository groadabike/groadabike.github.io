Professional Summary

Machine learning researcher specialising in deep learning for speech and audio, including speech separation, speech enhancement, and noise cancellation. PhD in Computer Science with a focus on speech and audio processing, and a track record of designing, implementing, and evaluating ML systems – from CNN and transformer-based models to full evaluation frameworks and benchmarks – for real-world audio applications, published in peer-reviewed venues. Experienced in translating research into deployable systems through cross-functional and industry collaboration.

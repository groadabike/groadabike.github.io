Automatic Lyrics Transcription from Single Audio Channel Polyphonic Music Recordings
Gerardo Roa Dabike

The Task

  • To recognise sung speech from an accompaniment singing.
  • Homophonic texture:
    • The common texture when lyrics need to be understood.
    • Contain one main melody and an accompaniment harmony.

Problem setup

  • Using unreleased video from CHiME-5 dataset
  • 20 unscripted parties taking place in different homes.
  • 4 participants in each party
  • 6 cameras in each home.
  • 2 cameras in each room.
  • Devices placed at the side of a room.
  • 114 recordings in total

Recording Device

  • Devices recorded using a Microsoft Kinect v2.
  • Camera is attached to a 4-channel microphone array.

Recording Device

    • 1080p camera
    • Field of view of 84.1 degrees
    • Mapping can be roughly approximated as linear
    \[\begin{aligned} pixel2angle(x) = \frac{x \times 84.1}{1920} \end{aligned} \]

    • Automatically Estimating positions

    • Pose Detection (Pose) OpenPose [1]
    • Face Detection (Face) Dlib [2]

    Annotating the data

    • Face+Mouth position
    • Tracking Demo not using CHiME-5 data.
    Tools available online: https://github.com/jackdeadman/video-annotation-tools

    Analysis of simulated datasets

    • SMS_WSJ [3]: Speaker separation sampled from uniform distribution
    • WSJ0-2Mix spatialised [4]: Speaker 3D positions in room randomly sampled with constraint on minimum distance
    • Proposed: Speaker location angles sampled from a normal distribution fit on the real data

    Analysis of simulated datasets

    Analysis of simulated datasets

    • WSJ0-original: Device in the centre of the room.
    • WSJ0-edges: Same but with the device placed at the edge of the room.
    • WSJ0-edges-realistic: devices at the edges with realistic positions for speakers.

    Conclusion

    • Speaker position is a variable not often discussed
    • Can have a large impact on the performance of speech enhancement and ASR
    • Argue that is should be a larger consideration incase algorithm that work only in simulations are persued just because they exploit easier speaker distributions
